The story behind the collection

About Uncommen

Exclusivity Preserved. Restocks Forbidden.

The Luxury of Being One in 100 Out of Millions

In an era governed by ubiquitous mass production, true individuality has become the ultimate luxury. Millions move through life enveloped in uniformity—wearing identical silhouettes, carrying repeated products, and surrounding themselves with choices dictated by the masses.

We forged Uncommen for the discerning few who refuse to conform.

Uncommen stands as a manifesto against the ordinary: dedicated to those who embody what is singular, rare, and un-repeatable.

Journeys Across India for the Unobtainable

To bring this vision to life, our specialized sourcing team travels to remote corners, heritage ateliers, and hidden artisanal hubs across India. They seek out master craftsmen and rare design methodologies that cannot be replicated by modern factories.

We curate these exceptional, obscure creations under an unyielding mandate: a maximum of 100 individually numbered pieces are ever brought into existence.

Our Absolute Promise

When you acquire a piece from us, that specific edition number is indelibly reserved for you. Once claimed, it transcends ordinary retail and enters the realm of the permanent vault.

While a product category or physical item type may eventually return to our store in a new form, a design once vaulted will NEVER be restocked or reproduced.

No reprints. No duplicates. No exceptions.

Some things in life—once gone—cannot be bought, regardless of price. By capping our releases so strictly, we guarantee that your acquisition remains a personal archetype, exclusively aligned with your journey.

We do not merely sell products; we are cultivating an elite community of connoisseurs who value true rarity, deep heritage, and unrepeatable sophistication.

The Uncommen Ethos

  • The Century Limit: Every design or artefact we unveil is strictly capped at precisely 100 iterations globally.
  • Exclusive Ownership: The moment your order is sealed, your unique serial number is permanently locked to your identity.
  • Vaulted in Perpetuity: Once the 100th piece of a design is claimed, it is archived forever—preserving your exclusivity for a lifetime.

Privacy Policy

Uncommen ("we," "us," or "our"), a sole proprietorship based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, is committed to safeguarding your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you visit our website or purchase our limited-edition creations.

1. Information We Collect

To fulfill your orders and deliver an exceptional experience, we collect personal information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact & Identity Information: Full name, phone number, email address, and delivery address.
  • Order & Serial Details: Purchases made, reserved edition/serial numbers assigned to your identity, and transaction history.
  • Payment Processing Data: Financial transactions are processed securely through our authorized payment partner, Razorpay. We do not store or process your credit/debit card details, UPI PINs, or net banking credentials on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize your personal information exclusively for operational and service excellence:

  • Processing, dispatching, and delivering your reserved limited-edition items.
  • Verifying and maintaining your unique serial number allocation.
  • Communicating order updates, delivery statuses, and customer support responses.
  • Complying with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations within India.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We value your trust and never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Your information is shared only with trusted third-party service providers (such as logistics partners and our payment gateway, Razorpay) strictly to complete your orders. We enforce stringent technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

4. Your Rights & Grievances

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored with us. For any privacy concerns, data inquiries, or grievances, please contact us at Contact@uncommen.in or reach us directly at +91 7051259897.

Customer-first support

Refund & Return Policy

At Uncommen, we place our patrons above everything else. While our creations are rare and strictly limited to 100 pieces, our commitment to your satisfaction is infinite. We stand proudly behind the quality of our craftsmanship and offer a customer-first approach to returns and refunds.

1. Return & Refund Eligibility

Because we value you more than the product itself, we gladly accept returns and process refunds under the following conditions:

  • You are unsatisfied with the product, or you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item.
  • The product must be returned in its original condition, unworn/unused, with all original tags, authentication cards, packaging, and assigned serial number tags intact.

2. 2-Day Reporting Window

To initiate a return or report a damaged/defective product, you must contact us within 2 business days of receiving your package.

How to Report: Email Contact@uncommen.in or message/call +91 7051259897 with your Order ID, details of the issue, and photos/videos of the item or defect.

3. Return Process & Refund Timeline

Once your return request is verified and approved, we will arrange a reverse pickup from your delivery address. Upon receiving and inspecting the returned item at our Bengaluru warehouse, we will initiate your refund.

  • Prepaid Orders: Refunds will be credited back to your original payment method via Razorpay within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds will be transferred directly to your provided bank account/UPI ID within 5 to 7 business days following inspection.

4. Order Cancellations

If you wish to cancel an order, you can do so anytime before dispatch for a full refund. Simply reach out immediately at +91 7051259897 or Contact@uncommen.in.
