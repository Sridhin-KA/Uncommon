Refund & Returns

Last updated: October 2026

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact us within 2 business days of delivery. Include your order number, a description of the issue and clear photographs of the item and packaging.

Return eligibility

Return requests are reviewed according to the product's condition and the applicable policy. Items should generally be unused, with original tags and packaging where applicable. Certain products may not be returnable where permitted by law. Please contact us before sending an item back.

How to request a return

  • Email Contact@uncommen.in.
  • Include your order number and reason for the request.
  • Wait for return instructions before shipping an item.

Refunds

Once an approved return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the outcome. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method where possible. Allow around 5–7 business days after processing for the amount to appear; bank and payment-provider timelines may differ.

Order cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel an order. Cancellation may not be possible after dispatch. If an order cannot be fulfilled, we will communicate the next steps and arrange an applicable refund.

Shipping charges
